Delivery & Return Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

🚚 Delivery

  • Free delivery within Tejgaon and Paikpara.
  • ৳80 elsewhere inside Dhaka city.
  • ৳140 outside Dhaka (all over Bangladesh).

The charge is calculated from the address you enter at checkout and the exact amount is shown before you confirm your order. Orders placed before 3 PM are usually prepared the same day, otherwise the next day. Delivery normally takes 1–3 days after dispatch. Our team calls you to confirm the delivery time before sending.

💳 Payment

We accept Cash on Delivery, bKash / Nagad / Upay "Send Money", and direct bank transfer. Mobile-wallet and bank payments are confirmed by our team using the transaction / reference number before your order is prepared.

Online payment: you can also pay online through the EPS (Easy Payment System) secure payment gateway:

  • bKash, Nagad, Rocket and other mobile financial services (MFS)
  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express (AMEX) and Nexus cards
  • Internet banking
  • EMI on eligible credit cards

Online payments are completed on the gateway's secure, PCI-DSS compliant page — we never see or store your card, PIN or OTP details. See our Privacy Policy.

↩️ Returns & Exchanges

We accept returns and exchanges for defective products: if an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or not as described.

  • Return window: tell us within 24 hours (1 day) of delivery, with your order code.
  • What you get: a replacement (exchange) of the same item, or a full refund if a replacement is not available.
  • Condition: the item must be unused and in its original packaging, with all accessories.
  • Cost: returns of defective or incorrect items are free — we arrange the replacement or refund at no extra charge.
  • Not covered: damage caused by misuse, or a change of mind after the item has been used.

For anything not covered above, message us — we'll work it out directly.

💸 Refunds

  • Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method (card, MFS wallet or bank account) within 7–10 working days of approval.
  • Card refunds may take longer to show on your statement, depending on your bank.
  • Cash on Delivery orders are refunded by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer.
  • If you cancel an order that has already been paid online, you get a full refund.
  • As required by Bangladesh Bank's escrow rule, online payments may be held by the payment gateway until delivery of your order is confirmed.
