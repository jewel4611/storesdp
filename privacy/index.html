Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Supreme Digital Point (shopsdp.netlify.app) is an online shop and marketplace based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you shop or sell with us, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

📋 What we collect

  • Customer details: your name, mobile number, e-mail address (if you give one) and delivery address.
  • Order history: the products you order, amounts, payment method, order status and delivery records.
  • Seller information (marketplace sellers only): shop name, owner name, contact details, business address, payout details and the products you list.
  • Technical information: basic device and browser details, pages visited and similar usage data collected by cookies and analytics tools (see below).

🎯 Why we use it

  • To process, pack and deliver your orders.
  • To provide customer support, handle returns, exchanges and refunds.
  • To send order updates — confirmation, dispatch and delivery — by SMS, WhatsApp or e-mail.
  • To prevent fraud and fake orders and to keep our shop and customers safe.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

🤝 Who we share it with

We share only the information each partner needs to do its job:

  • Courier partners (Steadfast, Pathao): your name, phone number, delivery address and the amount to collect, so they can deliver your parcel.
  • Payment gateway (EPS — Easy Payment System): the order amount and the details needed to process an online payment.
  • Marketplace sellers: when you buy a seller's product, the details needed to fulfil that order.
  • Authorities: only if the law requires it.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal data.

💳 Online payments

Online payments are processed on the payment gateway's secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment page. We never see or store your card number, PIN, OTP or wallet password. We only receive the payment result (success or failure), the amount and a transaction reference.

🍪 Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and browser storage to keep your cart, wishlist and sign-in working. We also use:

  • Google Analytics — to count visits and see which pages are useful.
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook) — to measure our ads and show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Meta Conversions API — when you place an order, our server tells Meta that a purchase happened (order value and product IDs) together with your mobile number and e-mail in hashed (scrambled, one-way) form, so Meta can match it to its own users without seeing the number. This only measures our ads; it is turned off when we switch the Pixel off.

Notifications. If you turn on Supreme Digital Point notifications, we also keep the items in your cart and wishlist (product IDs, names and prices — no personal details) linked to that notification, so we can remind you about an unfinished cart, or tell you when a saved item is back in stock or cheaper. Turning notifications off in your browser or app stops this.

These tools may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the shop will still work, but your cart may not be remembered. See how Google uses data and Meta's privacy policy.

🔒 Data security

Your information is stored on secure, access-controlled systems and sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Only our authorised staff can see order details, and only to serve your order.

🗓️ How long we keep data

We keep order and payment records for as long as needed to provide our service and to meet accounting, tax and legal requirements. Data that is no longer needed is deleted or made anonymous.

✋ Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your personal information. Contact us using the details below; we will reply within 7 working days. Some records (for example invoices) may have to be kept for legal reasons, but they will not be used for anything else.

🔄 Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
